## 💰 Monetization Strategy

### Phase 1: Free to Play (2026)
- All games completely free
- No ads
- Focus on growth and engagement

### Phase 2: Premium Features (Late 2026)
- **Free tier** - All games, basic features
- **Premium tier** ($4.99/month)
  - Ad-free experience
  - Exclusive skins and themes
  - Early access to new games
  - Advanced statistics
  - Cloud save across devices

- **Corporate training packages**
- **Custom game development**
